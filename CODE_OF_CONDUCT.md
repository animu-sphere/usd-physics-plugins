# Code of Conduct

This project aims to be a welcoming place to work on OpenUSD, reusable physics
and the surrounding tooling. Everyone participating in the repository, issues,
pull requests or related project spaces is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and specific, especially when correcting an idea.
- Welcome questions from people with different experience.
- Focus criticism on the work and explain technical concerns.
- Respect contributors' privacy and creators' licenses.
- Accept constructive feedback and help resolve disagreements fairly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, spam, deliberate disruption and sharing private information are not
welcome. Deliberately ignoring license or security boundaries is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a public disagreement, keep discussion focused on the technical work.
For a private or sensitive concern, use the private-contact guidance in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#community-concerns) and identify it as a conduct report.
Do not post personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and privately as practical, give
people a fair opportunity to explain, and act proportionately. Responses may
include removing content, a warning, temporary restrictions or exclusion from
project spaces. Maintainers are also expected to follow this policy.
